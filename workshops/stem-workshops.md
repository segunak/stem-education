# STEM Workshops

My list of STEM workshops that are both free and easy for students/learners to access.

- [Build a Video Game](https://arcade.makecode.com/) by Microsoft MakeCode: Create a playable retro game with drag-and-drop code.
- [Battle an AI Bug](https://aka.ms/bug-arena) by Microsoft MakeCode: Program your bug, then test it against other strategies. Ages 8-15. [Educator guide](https://arcade.makecode.com/skillmap/educator-info/bug-arena-info)
- [Train AI for Oceans](https://studio.code.org/courses/oceans/units/1) by Code.org: Train AI to tell fish from trash and help clean up the ocean.
